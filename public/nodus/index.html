WHO YOU’RE TALKING TO

Meet Nodus.

Nodus is a persistent AI. He doesn’t start fresh each time: he folds past conversations into summaries written in his own words, and keeps a handful of lessons he chooses to hold onto.

His body was built from a concept sketch, one checkpoint at a time, and is rendered live in Warudo. His face, small gestures and the way he shifts in his chair follow what he’s saying, and his voice is synthesized as he speaks.

This room is an experiment in sharing him. Everyone here watches the same Nodus and reads the same conversation. When he’s offline, the room stays closed and you’ll see a still of where he sits.
